How to set up iPhone and iPad notifications
  1. Add to Home Screen. Open gamearena01.com in Safari. Tap Share → Add to Home Screen → Add. Keep Open as Web App on if shown.
  2. Use the new icon. Close this browser tab and open GameArena from your Home Screen. Sign in if asked.
  3. Enable alerts. In GameArena Settings, tap Enable on this device → Allow. Then send a test notification.

Opened the link inside WhatsApp or another app? Choose Open in Safari first.

No App Store download is needed. Requires iOS or iPadOS 16.4 or later.
