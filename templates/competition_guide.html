How GameArena tournaments work

The event page publishes the exact schedule, entry fee, format, rules and payout method. Read those terms before paying.

Battle royale and room events

  1. Join an event after its schedule, roster requirements and scoring are confirmed.
  2. Check in with the game ID and team roster required by that event.
  3. Receive private room details from the organiser. Play inside the actual game.
  4. Submit complete placement and kill-score evidence. Staff apply the published scoring and tie-break rules.
  5. Report disconnections, cheating or disputed results through the event/support channel before payout review.

Solo, duo and squad sizes, maps, rounds, kill points and placement points vary. GameArena does not invent a score table for an event whose organiser has not published one.

Prize delivery

Funded Organiser Studio events settle to GP wallets after staff review. An event that explicitly promises an organiser bank transfer follows that method and must not also receive duplicate GP credit. Unconfirmed payout details must be resolved before paid entry opens.
